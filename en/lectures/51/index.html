Summary

A quadratic phase gives a Gaussian contribution for a real exponential and a Fresnel contribution for an imaginary exponential. The local formula needs a global estimate: a strict gap for Laplace’s method, and cancellation away from stationary points for stationary phase.

  • Orders: a limit and the dependence of constants are part of an error estimate. Relative errors require a nonzero approximation.

  • Laplace’s method: at a nondegenerate interior maximum, under the theorem’s other hypotheses (locally C3C^3C3 phase and C1C^1C1 amplitude, gap, weighted integrability), the leading term has an additive O ⁣(Λ−1)\bigO{\Lambda^{-1}}O(Λ−1) in the bracket multiplying eΛf(x0),\ee^{\Lambda f(x_0)}eΛf(x0​), for fixed data as Λ→+∞.\Lambda\to+\inftyΛ→+∞.
