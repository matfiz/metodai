Exercise 12.3 When the factors do not commute

Let A=(0100),B=(0010).A=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad B=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.A=(00​10​),B=(01​00​). Hint

The squares of the two matrices vanish, while (A+B)2=I.(A+B)^2=I(A+B)2=I. Group the even and odd powers in the last series.

Answer

The product is (2111),\left(\begin{smallmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)(21​11​), while the exponential of the sum is cosh⁡(1)I+sinh⁡(1)(A+B).\cosh(1)I+\sinh(1)(A+B)cosh(1)I+sinh(1)(A+B). The matrices fail to commute.
