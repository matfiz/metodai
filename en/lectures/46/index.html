Summary

The first variation differentiates a functional along every admissible direction. For a C2C^2C2 candidate, integration by parts and the fundamental lemma convert fixed-endpoint stationarity into Euler–Lagrange. First integrals reduce that equation, but their candidates still need verification in the original equation.

  • Admissibility: paths are C1C^1C1 with fixed endpoint values and (x,y,y′)(x,y,y')(x,y,y′) in U,UU, and directions are C1C^1C1 with zero endpoint values.

  • Local equation: a C2C^2C2 admissible path is stationary exactly when its Euler–Lagrange residual vanishes.

  • First integrals: missing yyy gives Fy′=k.F_{y'}=kFy′​=k. Missing xxx gives F−y′Fy′=k,F-y'F_{y'}=kF−y′Fy′​=k, with a converse check at constant intervals.
