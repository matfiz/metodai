Summary

A bead chain motivates a continuous displacement field. The small-slope string action gives the same forced equation as Lecture 29’s force balance. For a C2C^2C2 field, integration by parts and the fundamental lemma turn stationarity into an interior PDE and conditions on the free edges.

  • Field action: the density depends on u,uu, uxu_xux​ and ut.u_tut​. Each derivative term contributes its own pair of boundary edges.

  • String equation: for a C2C^2C2 field and continuous forcing, stationarity under directions vanishing on ∂R\partial R∂R is equivalent to ρutt=Tuxx+f\rho u_{tt}=Tu_{xx}+fρutt​=Tuxx​+f in the small-slope model.

  • Spatial ends: essential conditions prescribe displacement and restrict the directions. A free massless end gives the natural condition ux=0.u_x=0ux​=0.

  • Temporal edges: fixed configurations remove temporal boundary terms. Cauchy displacement and velocity are prescribed separately.
