Exercise 2.6 Circulation around an excluded axis

For A=Ce^φ/r\vv A=C\unitvec{\varphi}/rA=Ceφ​/r with C∈R∖{0},C\in\R\setminus\{0\}C∈R∖{0}, find divergence and curl on r>0.r>0r>0. The units of CCC are field units multiplied by length. A scalar potential is a C1C^1C1 function ψ\psiψ satisfying ∇ψ=A.\grad\psi=\vv A∇ψ=A. Find such a potential on an angular patch. Prove that no single-valued C1C^1C1 potential exists on all of R3\R^3R3 with the zzz-axis removed, and explain why Stokes’ theorem is consistent with the circulation.

Hint

Integrate the angular physical gradient component locally to obtain a potential. For a global potential, apply the chain rule around a closed parametrized loop.

Answer

Divergence and curl vanish off the axis. Circulation is 2πC2\pi C2πC and a local potential is Cφ.C\varphiCφ. A global single-valued potential would give zero circulation around every closed loop, which contradicts this value.
